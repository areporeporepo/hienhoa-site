How orders work

  1. Choose products and quantities, sign in, and submit your request with a reply email. Keep your request reference.
  2. Hiển Hòa reviews the request and issues a quote with its currency, subtotal, shipping, tax, delivery and payment terms, and expiry.
  3. Open your order history. Review and accept the current quote to confirm the order. Payment is handled separately under the merchant’s quoted terms.

Hienhoa’s ChatGPT integration connects the catalog and order form to the same order system used by the merchant. Your account lets you retrieve your own requests, quotes and confirmations. Hiển Hòa confirms delivery availability for the destination you request.
